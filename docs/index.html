Battery management systems · edge computing

Live State-of-Charge Estimation at the Edge

A Raspberry Pi 5 takes Li-ion cell measurements (a replayed laboratory drive cycle, or a smart battery read over SMBus), runs four state-of-charge estimators on the device, and streams the measurements and estimates to this page through a cloud MQTT broker.
