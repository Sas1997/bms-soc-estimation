Live state-of-charge estimation at the edge

A single-board computer takes Li-ion cell measurements (a replayed laboratory drive cycle, or a smart battery read over SMBus), estimates state of charge on the device, and streams measurements and estimates here over MQTT. Code and method: bms-soc-estimation.
